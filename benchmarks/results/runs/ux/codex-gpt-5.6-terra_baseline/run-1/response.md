A confirmation modal helps a little, but it’s not sufficient—people habituate to “Are you sure?”

Make accidental deletion recoverable by default:

- Soft-delete workspaces with a clear 30-day recovery window.
- Show an immediate **Undo** after deletion.
- Require typing the workspace name only for irreversible/purge actions.
- Make “Delete permanently” visually distinct and never the default.

That turns a panic-inducing mistake into a reversible action, rather than relying on users to read one more modal.
