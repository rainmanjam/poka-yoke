That approach was already explored earlier and rejected — users ignore frequent confirmation modals, especially for high-intent actions like deletes. The modal won't solve the panic emails.

Instead, the recommendation from before was: **soft-delete + 30-day trash + undo toast**. Users get an immediate undo ("Workspace deleted — Undo?"), the workspace sits in trash for 30 days, and they can restore it if they realize the mistake. Far less support burden than a modal they'll just click through.
